    How Age is Calculated

    Our Next-Gen Age Calculator uses precise calendar-based calculations that account for leap years, different month lengths, and exact day differences. This ensures highly accurate age results.

    Instead of simply subtracting years, the system calculates the true time difference between your birth date and the selected date.
